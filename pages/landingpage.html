ABOUT SCANJO

Your healthcare journey, made simpler.

Finding the right medical service shouldn't be complicated.

ScanJo is designed to help people across Jordan navigate their healthcare journey with greater clarity and confidence. From finding the right medical service and exploring available appointments to understanding preparation requirements, we bring essential information together in one place.

Why we created ScanJo

We believe patients deserve a clearer picture of what comes next. Finding the right facility, understanding preparation instructions, and keeping track of the steps before a medical procedure can be confusing and time-consuming.

Our mission

To make healthcare services easier to find, medical preparation easier to understand, and the patient journey more organized for everyone in Jordan.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

What is ScanJO?

ScanJO is a simple healthcare tool that helps you identify the right diagnostic scan, compare centers, and take the next step with more confidence.

How do I find the right scan?

You can start by exploring the available scan types or uploading a referral, then compare nearby centers based on location and services.

Can I compare healthcare centers?

Yes. ScanJO makes it easier to compare clinics and imaging centers in one place so you can choose the option that best fits your needs.

Is ScanJO free to use?

Yes, the platform is designed to help patients explore scan and center information quickly and with no extra hassle.
